## 6. The long tail — caption/alt PATTERN (not 412 captions)

The catalog pass fills the remaining pieces from data using these patterns. The patterns are built so a correct caption can be written from catalog fields alone — and so a missing field degrades the caption honestly instead of inventing one.

### 6.1 Dahlias (the other 398)

- **Title:** the variety name. Nothing else, ever — no filename, no series name, no invented epithet.
- **captionShort pattern:** `{Color as photographed} {form, if the catalog carries it}` — one short noun phrase, written from the photograph or from confirmed variety data, whichever the catalog pass actually has. If the catalog has neither confirmed color nor form for a variety, captionShort is the fallback line: **One of the {412} cultivar photographs.** — true of every piece in the wing, and better than a guess wearing a caption's clothes.
- **captionLong:** none, unless a specific piece carries a documented story (a site lead, a recorded note). The default is absence (Nadia §4.3).
- **altText pattern:** `A {Variety} dahlia: {what the photograph shows — color, form, framing}.`

2. **Ghislaine** (game-pool photograph — the pattern's provenance discipline on show):
   - captionShort: An apricot and salmon ball.
   - altText: A Ghislaine dahlia: an apricot and salmon ball-form bloom.

### 6.2 Park, Art & Motifs, Arcade long tail

- **Park photographs:** captionShort names what is in the frame and where in the park it is, only where the catalog knows both; altText follows the same content. Group (Beds & Blooms / The Grounds / Seasons / People) is catalog data.
- **Botanical plates:** captionShort pattern: **A whole-plant plate — {the plant as painted}.** altText pattern: **Botanical watercolor plate of {plant}: {blooms/foliage above, roots/tubers below where shown}, on paper.** Set name rides in `group`: Botanical plates.
- **Site paintings:** captionShort pattern: **The {page or place it was painted for} painting.** — only where `whereUsed` is actually known; otherwise a plain description of the scene. Never invent a commission story.
- **Arcade pieces:** captionShort pattern: **Finished art from {game}.** where the catalog names the game; pieces in More from the Arcade get a plain description of what the art shows, and no game is invented for them (Nadia §3.5).
- **Sorting Table components, game pieces, tab-bar art, spot motifs, working files:** not exhibits (Elena §5) — no copy is written for them, and none should be added at catalog time without coming back through Hue.

### 6.3 Tracks (the 17 undocumented songs — and any track without an authored caption)

Per Nadia's amended §4.3 track-scope ruling (gate F12), a track's `captionShort` is **pattern-supplied, not authored**: a track exhibits on `title` + `voice` + `durationSec` (+ `companionSlug` where paired), and this pattern builds its one line from those fields alone. It states what the recording is, how long it runs, and what its pairing is — nothing more, because the record holds nothing more for these songs (G2): no story, no lyrics, no dates are claimed or implied. The two highlight songs keep their authored captions (§5.4); this pattern serves the other 17, and any track whose authored caption is ever absent.

- **Paired — this recording sung:** The sung recording — {duration}. The instrumental recording is on this page.
- **Paired — this recording instrumental:** The instrumental recording — {duration}. The sung recording is on this page.
- **Unpaired — sung:** The sung recording — {duration}. No second recording of this song is in the collection.
- **Unpaired — instrumental:** The instrumental recording — {duration}. No sung recording of this song is in the collection.

**Degradation, coordinated with G9/F6:** the pairing clause renders **only** where `companionSlug` exists — the same condition that governs the §4 framing line and the version switch. An unpaired song never claims a companion in any slot: no framing line, no switch, and the pattern's second sentence says plainly that there is no second recording, instead of staying silent and letting the room's two-recordings rhythm imply one.

**Three worked examples** (titles, voices and durations from the Music page record; nothing beyond them is claimed for any of these songs):

   - Sung captionShort: The sung recording — 3:12. The instrumental recording is on this page.
   - Instrumental captionShort: The instrumental recording — 3:04. The sung recording is on this page.
2. **September at Hues** (sung only in the record — 4:35, no instrumental counterpart):
   - captionShort: The sung recording — 4:35. No second recording of this song is in the collection.
3. **Rows of Color** (instrumental only in the record — 2:53, no sung counterpart):
   - captionShort: The instrumental recording — 2:53. No sung recording of this song is in the collection.
